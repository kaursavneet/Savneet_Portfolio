Product Description

The Gibson Limited Edition Les Paul 60s Standard Electric Guitar in Transparent Wine Red is a stunning tribute to the golden age of rock. Featuring a solid mahogany body topped with a beautifully figured maple cap, this guitar delivers rich resonance and classic sustain. The SlimTaper mahogany neck and bound rosewood fingerboard offer smooth playability, while Burstbucker 61R and 61T humbuckers provide vintage-inspired tones with modern clarity. Hand-wired electronics with orange drop capacitors ensure dynamic response and tonal versatility. Finished in a luxurious Transparent Wine Red gloss, this limited edition Les Paul combines timeless style, premium craftsmanship, and legendary sound for discerning players and collectors alike.
