ToneMaster Deluxe Reverb Combo Amplifier

Product information

The Fender Tone Master Deluxe Reverb Combo Amplifier is a revolutionary blend of vintage tone and modern technology. Using advanced digital modeling, it faithfully replicates the iconic sound and dynamic response of the original 22-watt tube Deluxe Reverb, while delivering 100 watts of solid-state power. Housed in a lightweight solid pine cabinet and equipped with a Jensen N-12K neodymium speaker, this amp offers sparkling cleans, rich reverb, and lush tremolo with half the weight of its tube counterpart. It features Normal and Vibrato channels, a 6-way power attenuator for flexible volume control, and a balanced XLR output with cabinet IR simulations for silent recording or live performance. With USB connectivity for firmware updates and a two-button footswitch included, the Tone Master Deluxe Reverb is perfect for gigging musicians who demand classic tone, portability, and versatility.
