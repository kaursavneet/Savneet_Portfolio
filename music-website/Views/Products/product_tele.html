Fender

American Professional II Telecaster

SKU: #73723736 | Model: #011-3942-750 | ★★★★★

Product Information

The Fender American Professional II Telecaster is a modern evolution of a legendary classic, designed to meet the demands of today’s discerning players. Featuring a lightweight alder or roasted pine body, this Telecaster delivers resonant tone with balanced highs and tight lows. Its Deep “C” maple neck with rolled fingerboard edges and a “Super-Natural” satin finish offers exceptional comfort and effortless playability. The sculpted neck heel ensures smooth access to upper frets, while 22 narrow-tall frets enhance precision and bending. Equipped with V-Mod II single-coil pickups, it delivers articulate tones with the iconic Tele twang, snap, and snarl. A top-load/string-through bridge with compensated brass “bullet” saddles provides excellent intonation and tonal flexibility. With its refined design and versatile sound, the American Professional II Telecaster sets a new standard for performance and reliability.
