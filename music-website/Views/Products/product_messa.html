Mesa Boogie

Rectifier Badlander 50W Head

SKU: #741629 | Model: #2.BD5.3.BB | ★★★★★

Product Information

The Mesa Boogie Rectifier Badlander 50W Head is a modern evolution of the iconic Rectifier series, delivering aggressive tone and versatile performance in a compact, gig-ready format. Featuring two fully independent channels with three selectable modes—Clean, Crunch, and Crush—this amp offers a wide tonal range from pristine cleans to saturated high-gain distortion. Powered by EL34 tubes (with optional 6L6s), it includes a Multi-Watt switch for toggling between 50W and 20W output, perfect for stage or studio. The built-in CabClone IR provides eight Mesa cabinet simulations and third-party IR support, enabling silent recording or direct output with authentic tone. Additional features include a Bold/Variac switch, bias select, effects loop, headphone output, and a footswitch. With its tight lows, punchy mids, and singing highs, the Badlander 50 is a fire-breathing amp head built for players who demand flexibility, portability, and uncompromising sound.
