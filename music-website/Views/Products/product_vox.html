AC15C1 - 15 Watt 1x12 Tube Combo

Product Information

The Vox AC15C1 is a legendary 15-watt tube combo amplifier that delivers the unmistakable British tone that defined generations of rock music. Featuring two distinct channels—Normal and Top Boost—this amp offers a wide range of tonal possibilities, from sparkling cleans to rich, overdriven textures. Powered by three 12AX7 preamp tubes and two EL84 power tubes, it drives a 12" Celestion G12M Greenback speaker for warm, articulate sound. Classic onboard effects include a lush spring reverb and a vintage-style tremolo with adjustable speed and depth. The AC15C1 also includes master Tone Cut and Volume controls for precise gain staging, and external speaker outputs for expanded rig options. With its iconic chime, dynamic response, and timeless design, the AC15C1 is a favorite among studio musicians and live performers alike.
