SG Standard Electric Guitar - Heritage Cherry

The Gibson SG Standard Electric Guitar in Heritage Cherry is a timeless classic that blends vintage style with modern performance. Crafted from solid mahogany, its lightweight double-cutaway body offers exceptional comfort and access to higher frets, making it ideal for fast, expressive playing. The rounded mahogany neck and bound rosewood fingerboard provide a smooth, familiar feel, while the dual 490R and 490T humbuckers deliver rich, balanced tones with plenty of bite and sustain. Chrome hardware, a Tune-O-Matic bridge, and Grover tuners ensure solid tuning stability. Finished in a striking Heritage Cherry gloss, this SG Standard is a must-have for players seeking iconic tone and style. A true icon of rock history, the Gibson SG Standard in Heritage Cherry delivers legendary tone, effortless playability, and timeless style. Revered by generations of guitarists—from Iommi to Young—this modern classic is built to inspire.
